# Research: process, anniversary marks, presentation, portfolio

Gathered 5 Nov 2026. Most sources are studio blogs and published brand guides, so they show common practice rather than fixed rules.

## 1 · What we know about the client's identity
- **Constant:** the round quartered emblem: roti, tools, tap and heart.
- **Anniversary pattern:** the emblem becomes the round part of the number. In 9 Years ("Years of Change", 2025) it is the bowl of the 9. In 10 Years ("Decade of Change", 2026) it is the 0. **11 has no round digit**, so the series' own logic breaks here. That is the core design problem and the story to tell.
- **Inconsistent:** the wordmark (four or more styles), the sign colours, and the lack of any usage rules.
- **Not theirs:** the orange two-hands-and-heart icon and the orange accents come from the Lenity WordPress theme.
- **Preferences seen:** deep green and gold, white, bold heavy sans for HAMMAD / FOUNDATION, Urdu calligraphy in gold, and celebratory "Years of / Decade of Change" taglines.

## 2 · Anniversary marks: lessons
- **Precedent for their approach:** Massimo Vignelli's 100th-anniversary mark for Abdi İbrahim drew the "0" as the company's emblem. A published study praises it because the anniversary feels part of the brand, and contrasts it with Anadolu University's 50th, where the number was simply placed under the logo ([JECS paper](https://jecs.pl/index.php/jecs/article/view/645/505)). Use this in the presentation to show that the 11 Years mark continues a respected tradition.
- **Pentagram, MTA Arts & Design 40th** ([link](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mta-arts-design-40th), Giorgia Lupi), shared by Hammad. What we can borrow:
  - **Extend the existing mark's geometry.** The "40" is a monoline numeral built from the rhythm of the logo's two circles, and the 0 becomes a third circle holding "Since 1985". Our version: keep the 11 as clean as the doorways, and test a small "Since 2015/16" line inside or under the mark once the founding year is confirmed.
  - **One anniversary accent colour.** They added a violet for the year. Ours could be gold, used as a single flat colour plus one gradient for digital.
  - **A campaign line with a two-beat rhythm.** "Art Everywhere for Everyone" echoes the two circles and the 4 and 0. Our 11 is two beats too, so a two-part Urdu or English line fits, e.g. *hath mai hath* / *Decade → Journey*. Confirm with the media team.
  - **Motion as part of the identity.** The 40 draws on in one stroke and the 0 closes around the date. Ours could have the doorways open and light pour through, for social posts and the website.
  - **Applications shown (screenshots in `reference/inspiration/pentagram-mta-40/`):** a hero motion film; a subway digital poster with the campaign line and the lockup small in the footer; a tote with a one-colour white lockup on violet; an event screen; a poster triptych; a sticker sheet in violet and white. The lockup is always *the master logo plus the anniversary mark together*.
  - **Page structure:** title and one-line descriptor, then story copy (background, construction, colour, line, motion, applications), then credits. Short, confident, mostly visual.
- **Lockups:** most guides define the anniversary mark plus the master logo in horizontal and stacked forms, separated by a divider, with the master logo left unchanged.
  - [Detroit Mercy 150 guide](https://www.udmercy.edu/150/files/DM-150-Brand-Guidelines-2026-FINAL.pdf)
  - [MCC 20th anniversary guide](https://www.mcc.gov/resources/doc/20th-anniv-branding-guidelines/)
  - [Singapore SYTWL style guide](https://cgs.gov.sg/files/Say%20YES%20to%20Waste%20Less/SYTWL_Style_Guide_30Aug_compressed.pdf)
- **Temporary by design:** anniversary marks have a start date and an end date ([LSU](https://itservice.lsu.edu/TDClient/51/OCUR/KB/PrintArticle?ID=453)). State "January–December 2027" in the guidelines.
- **Use it as a system, not a sticker:** the mark should carry through marketing, wayfinding and social campaigns ([Zebradog](https://zebradog.com/insights/brand-identity-anniversary-logo-design)).
- More guides worth skimming:
  - [Worcester State 150th](https://webcdn.worcester.edu/mywoostate-facstaff/wp-content/uploads/sites/67/2024/05/150th-Anniversary-Visual-Identity-Guidelines.pdf)
  - [Bread for the World 50th brand book](https://www.bread.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Bread-50th-Brand-Book.pdf)
  - [Georgetown 175th](https://issuu.com/localitystudio/docs/georgetown175_brandguidelines)
  - [U Iowa anniversary logo](https://brand.uiowa.edu/anniversary-logo)

## 3 · Presenting the logo (PDF structure)
Principles that recur across sources:
- Open by restating the brief.
- Tell the work as a story: research → insight → concept → rationale → system → applications.
- Let the core mark land before you show any merchandise.
- Show real-world mockups.
- Answer objections by tying each choice back to the brief.

Sources: [DesignRush](https://www.designrush.com/best-designs/logo/trends/how-to-present-a-logo-design-to-a-client), [Clay](https://clay.global/blog/how-to-present-a-logo), [Fabrik](https://fabrikbrands.com/branding-matters/logo-design/how-to-present-a-logo-to-a-client/), [Studio Function](https://medium.com/studio-function/logo-design-guide-4-of-5-notes-on-presenting-e1c130974dd0), [Wannathis](https://wannathis.one/blog/how-to-present-a-brand-identity-to-a-client).

**Our PDF (16:9, about 18 pages):**
1. Cover: the mark on deep green
2. The brief, in one line
3. Nine, ten… eleven: the series and why 11 breaks it
4. Insight: 11 has no circle, so the emblem needs a new role
5. Concept story: one sentence and one image
6. The mark: large, on white
7. Construction and proportions
8. English and Urdu versions: 11 Years / ۱۱ سال
9. Colour: specs for print and screen
10. Variations: horizontal, stacked, icon, one-colour, reversed
11. Lockup with the master logo
12. Clear space, minimum size, misuse
13. Backgrounds: the grid
14–17. Applications: website and social, T-shirt and vest, signboard and banner, billboard
18. File list and thank you, with contact details

For a competition, submit **one** primary direction with confidence. Add an alternate only if it is clearly different and equally strong.

## 4 · Portfolio case study
Behance frames a case study as a story: explain *why* as well as *what*. Draft it in a doc first, then lay it out ([Behance](https://www.behance.net/resources/articles/building-the-perfect-case-study)). Typical structure: context → challenge → process → final system → applications → outcome. Use 8–15 images at 1400 px wide or more ([Unilink](https://app.unilink.us/blog/best-behance-projects-2026)).

**Our case study:**
1. Title and one-line context (Karachi NGO, 11th anniversary, open competition)
2. Challenge: the 9/10 pattern and the "110" trap
3. Research: site audit, logo history, field photos
4. Exploration: nine directions, including the archived ones and why they were dropped
5. Breakthrough: the doorway and the bilingual ۱۱ (if chosen)
6. Final mark and system
7. Applications
8. Outcome: submitted / selected, plus what was learned

**Collect as we go:** sketch scans, board screenshots at each step, the decision log (`CONTEXT.md`), and before/after pairs.

## 6 · Production specs (for printers)
- Vector files are required for screen print, embroidery and engraving.
- Raster files at 300 dpi at final size.
- Pantone for screen print; CMYK for full-colour print.
- No gradients in embroidery or one-colour print.
- Embroidery text cap height 4 mm or more (about 0.25 in).

Check every figure with the actual printer ([A4 Apparel](https://js.a4apparel.co.uk/shirt%20printers/design-guide.html), [Ebaq: logo files](https://www.ebaqdesign.com/blog/logo-files), [ArtVersion checklist](https://artversion.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Brand-Identity-Design-Checklist-ArtVersion.pdf)).
