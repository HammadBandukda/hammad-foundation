## 2 · Strategy: concept criteria 🟡
**Drafted in `docs/PROBLEM.md`:** audit, problems P1–P5, problem statement, criteria C1–C12, score sheet, and the system-thinking page. The list below is the original summary.
- **Must:**
  1. Reads as **11**, never 110 or 101.
  2. Keeps the emblem unchanged (same quadrants, icons and greens).
  3. Belongs to the 9 and 10 Years series (gold and green, a "Years" device).
  4. Legible at 16 px (favicon) and 70 px (header).
  5. Works in one colour (screen print, embroidery, hi-vis vests).
  6. Holds up on all nine test backgrounds (Figma 04).
- **Should:**
  7. Tells a story tied to their mission (dignity, growth, "Transforming Pakistan by 2050").
  8. Optionally reads in Urdu (۱۱ / سال).
  9. Has a lockup that works next to the master logo.
- **Out:** a criteria sheet plus the one-sentence story the mark tells.
- **Gate:** Hammad approves the criteria.
- **Capture:** the criteria sheet.

- **Gate:** at least two directions meet every **Must** in step 2.

## 4 · Selection ⬜
- Score the shortlist against the criteria, then pick **one primary** direction and at most **one alternate**.
- **Gate:** a written decision with its reason.
- **Capture:** the scoring table.

## 5 · Refinement ⬜
- Construction grid and proportions
- Custom-drawn numerals, with no off-the-shelf font for the "11"
- The ribbon word (Years / سال): size and lettering
- Colour specs: HEX, RGB, CMYK and a Pantone match for the greens and gold, plus a flat gold for print
- **Gate:** the mark passes the 16 px, one-colour and nine-background tests again.
- **Capture:** before/after comparison and a construction diagram.

## 6 · System ⬜
- **Variations:** primary, horizontal (website header), stacked, icon/favicon, social avatar.
- **Colour versions:** full colour, one colour (deep green, black, white), reversed.
- **Languages:** English, and Urdu if kept.
- **Lockup with the master logo:** position, divider, size ratio.
- **Rules:** clear space (based on the emblem's ring), minimum sizes (print mm and screen px), misuse examples.
- **Gate:** every application in `docs/CHECKLIST.md` §A has a matching variation.

## 6b · Logo animation ⬜ (final mark only)
- Start only after Step 6. Never animate exploration concepts.
- A 3–5 s reveal: the 11 draws in or the doors open, light comes through, the emblem settles, then the ribbon and the words.
- **Out:** MP4 (1080×1080 and 1920×1080), a transparent version (WebM, or a GIF for WhatsApp), a 9:16 story cut, and a static end frame.
- **Tools:** After Effects (from the Illustrator layers) or Figma Smart Animate for the storyboard.
- **Gate:** it works on mute, holds a 1 s end frame, and is under 5 MB for social.

## 7 · Applications ⬜
- Mock up the priority list in `docs/CHECKLIST.md` §A.
- Use licensed or self-made mockups only, and record the source of each.
- **Gate:** at least 8 strong mockups that cover digital, print, apparel and signage.
- **Capture:** all mockups exported at 1400 px wide or more.

## 8 · Usage guidelines ⬜
- 4–6 pages: variations, colours, clear space, minimum size, lockup, do's and don'ts, timeframe (2027 only).
- **Gate:** a printer or volunteer could apply the mark correctly from these pages alone.

## 9 · Presentation PDF ⬜
- **Template ready:** Figma section 07 (`3075:3953`), 21 slides with placeholders. Replace the placeholders as each step finishes.
- Use the outline in `docs/RESEARCH.md` §3.
- **Gate:** it reads as a story without any voice-over, and is under 15 MB.

## 10 · Production files ⬜
- Rebuild in Illustrator with clean paths and named layers.
- Convert all type to outlines.
- Set up colour swatches: CMYK, plus Pantone spot colours for print.
- Export: AI, PDF, EPS and SVG for print; PNG (transparent, @1x/@2x/@4x) for screens; ICO/PNG favicons.
- **Gate:** every file in the export checklist (`docs/CHECKLIST.md` §C) is present and opens correctly.

## 11 · Submission ⬜
- Email media@hammadfoundation.com: the PDF, plus PNG previews attached and a link to the vector files.
- Target date: 18 Nov.
- **Gate:** sent, and the files are confirmed received.
