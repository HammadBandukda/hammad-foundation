# Hammad Foundation goals

A goals list in the style of the UN SDG communications material (one number, name, icon and colour per goal), built from what the foundation publishes on hammadfoundation.com, its signage and its projects page (Nov 2026).

**This is our reading, not an official list.** Confirm it with the media team (see `PROBLEM.md` §6). Urdu names need a native check before use.

| # | Goal | اردو | Emblem icon | Programmes | Published impact | SDG |

| 1 | **Food**: no one sleeps hungry | خوراک | Stacked roti | Roti Bank, Ramadan Bachat Camp (Rs 10/kg produce) | 100 affordable roti daily | 2 Zero Hunger |

## Cross-cutting focus areas (chairman's message)
Women empowerment · Youth empowerment · Political awareness · Social responsibility · Climate change.

## The umbrella
- **Vision:** "Transforming Pakistan by 2050".
- **Mission line:** "From Aid to Ability". "We promote a buying culture, not a begging culture."
- **Hashtag:** #hath_mai_hath_do_hammad_ka_sath_do

## How this feeds the design
- **System-thinking page (`PROBLEM.md` §5):** goals 1–4 become the four pillar icons cut from the emblem. Goals 5–8 show the extension question for the media team: new icons, or grouped under an existing pillar?
